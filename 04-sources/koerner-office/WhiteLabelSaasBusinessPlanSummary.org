* Core model

GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one agency CRM (pipelines, sites, calendars, automations, reputation, invoicing). List price to agencies/businesses: about *$97–$497/month*.

White-label mode: pay GHL a *flat $300–$500/month* that *does not scale with customer count*. Rebrand it, pick a niche, set your price, sell it.

Infrastructure = GHL. You supply niche, brand, and distribution.

* Diamond Award (how the plan spots $100k+/month shops)

Public GHL badge. Minimum: *1,000 paying monthly recurring customers*.

At a conservative ~$100/month average that is *≥ $100k/month* recurring. Most winners are higher. Plan's other platform stats: 70,000+ agencies on GHL; SaaS marketplace ~229 apps; GHL's own non-WL CRM plan $97/month.

* Two paths

- *Path A — niche white-label:* pick an industry you already know; wrap GHL; sell monthly to that trade.
- *Path B — GHL add-on:* sell a tool *to people already on GHL* (Zappy Chat). Distribution is the GHL marketplace / user base, not cold industry outreach.

* Pricing rule the plan will not bend

Monthly recurring. ClickSlow is the negative proof. Setup fees are allowed (Push Press $700) as long as the spine is MRR.

* Niche-finding pattern

Every winner was *already inside* the trade. Pain points first, software second. Plan's line: the software is GHL; the unfair advantage is the job you already had (notary, gym desk, tutor, insurance).

Example mapping table in the plan: notaries → calendar+CRM; gyms → check-in+billing; tutors → leads+scheduling; IUL → pipeline+docs+training; also sketched: dog groomers, wedding photographers.

* Acquisition without an audience (plan)

Partner with a creator or trainer *already trusted in the niche* (notary course, gym coach, insurance community). Copy-paste their distribution rather than growing a personal brand from zero.

* Six reverse-engineering tools named

SpyFu, Wappalyzer, publicwww.com, PredictLeads, Apollo.io, TheirStack.com.

Use: see what a Diamond shop runs, what they bid on, what stack their customers show, then clone structure not trademark.

* 30-day launch (plan's close)

Pick one niche you already understand. Partner with one creator. Stand up the white-label. Sell monthly. Do not write software. Do not price one-time.

Closing slogan in the source: "Pick a Niche. Partner With a Creator. Copy Paste."

* Lock-in mechanic

Get the customer to store *their* clients, list, calendar, and automations in your wrapper. Leaving means leaving their books. IUL Insiders is the extreme version (community + courses + CRM).

* Relation to other 2026-09-02 work

- Porch pumpkin plan is a *seasonal physical* business with upfront pay and a two-month sprint. This SaaS plan is *year-round MRR* on rented software.
- Only weak overlap: if a porch-pumpkin operator later wanted a booking/CRM wrapper, GHL is one off-the-shelf option. Do not merge the two projects.

* Caveats

- Diamond math is a *floor at 1,000 customers × assumed ARPU*, not audited revenue.
- GHL pricing and WL fee change; confirm on GHL's site before modeling.
- White-label still requires support, niche copy, and payment processing. Flat $300–$500 is software rent, not total COGS.
