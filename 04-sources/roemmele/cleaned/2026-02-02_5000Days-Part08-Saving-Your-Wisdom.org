* Summary

Part 8 (2 Feb 2026) is the series’ first full treatment of [[SaveYourWisdom]]. After recapping Parts 1–7 as a Joseph Campbell monomyth (Call through Resurrection), Roemmele names SaveWisdom.org as the elixir the hero brings home: preserve experiential judgment before wage-work dissolves, then encode it so a *local* AI can amplify it.

The public method: answer up to 1000 curated questions on a cheap offline recorder; keep audio; transcribe; build a private model that “knows” you. He contrasts knowledge (what machines already do) with wisdom (ethical residue of a life). The Noosphere / omega-point talk is the optional later sharing layer, only with permission.

A long middle section argues that the *act of answering* is narrative therapy (White & Epston): externalize problems, thicken preferred stories, find unique outcomes. He calls that side-effect possibly larger than the future “wisdom keeper” model. The 1000 questions are then mapped onto the monomyth itself (Ordinary World → Return).

Member-only close on this capture is a placeholder, not a second essay.

* Key Claims

- In the 5000-day interregnum the scarce good is wisdom, not labor.
- SaveWisdom.org is the series’ “elixir”: capture → transcript → local model that knows the person.
- Questions are a scaffold, not a sacrament; voice and emotion are part of the data.
- AI fed only facts stays an efficiency engine; AI fed lived judgment becomes an “Intelligence Amplifier.”
- Civilizational amnesia (Alexandria, lost oral traditions) is the warning analog for a digital dark age.
- Narrative therapy is an intended-or-at-least-welcomed side-effect and may be the bigger gift *now*.
- Private hoard plus optional shared noosphere: he is explicit that those who keep a private wisdom store will fare better than those who only consume homogenized AI output.
- Cost bar is a ~$30 recorder; no subscription required for capture.
- Technical how-to for the model is pointed at, not specified in this part (matches savewisdom.org “forthcoming articles”).

* Major Sections

** Series recap as monomyth
Parts 1–7 mapped: Call (Abundance Interregnum), Refusal, Player Piano / deskilling, mentor reframing, inmost cave, Dark Night, Consider Phlebas / resurrection. Part 8 = Road Back / Elixir.

** Why SaveWisdom.org now
“Who will we be?” not “What will we do?” Offline 1000-question voice archive as anchor while agents write code and run logistics. Wisdom as the human differentiator against the player-piano fate.

** Capture → local model
Voice recordings become written testimonies via transcription, then “secure, local AI models that know you.” Family heirloom (Grandma’s voice) and optional collective noosphere are both named. Not silicon immortality — legacy.

** Narrative therapy overlay
Cites *Narrative Means to Therapeutic Ends*. Answering aloud re-authors the life, externalizes problems, surfaces exceptions. He ranks this present-tense shift above the later model.

** 1000 questions as personal monomyth
Origins / family = Ordinary World; decisions = Call; regrets = Refusal; hardships = Trials; teachers = Mentor; grief = Ordeal; triumphs = Reward; legacy = Return. Speaking it makes it usable as model training text.

** Closing charge
Start recording; build the companion; next public series beat pointed at Part 10 (books on machine–human partnership). Part 9 (Artisan’s Awakening) is the chronological next essay.
