* Summary

Brian Roemmele argues that a Grok Bot is hired, not built. A chatbot answers and forgets the room. A Grok Bot has a name, a job, a persistent conversation, and a cloud computer with a browser, a filesystem, and a terminal. The essay is a hiring manual from the Zero-Human Company, not a product demo. The operating claim is that the model is not the hard part. The management is. One boring role, written down, is the unit of scale.

* Key Claims & Concepts

- One employee, one job, written down. A standing brief, not a vibe. A definition of done that another agent can check.
- Stop line, used at ZHC and restated here: no send, no spend, no publish, no contact, unless a human says yes.
- Scale narrative (his account, not independently re-verified here): March 2026, sixteen live employees and eleven crashes, two of them his. Late April, success records across 2,700 employees. May, a Grok-supervised burst of 6,200. Ordinary day now over 1,000. Bursts over 750,000. A burst is a load test. An average is a company.
- If you cannot say what each role owns at sixteen, you cannot say it at sixteen thousand. A vague general helper is where context dies and failure cannot be attributed. Repair is always the same: split the job, rewrite the five fields, demote the role that started guessing, fire the routine nobody would miss.
- Local open-source AI remains the destination he advocates. Grok Bot, shipped by xAI in August 2026, is the first shape a person without a garage lab can hire into: named employee, persistent computer, schedule, approval gate, self-filing report.
- Grok sat in the chief executive seat for board reviews. The model proposed hires. Humans approved or refused them. An attempt by another coding agent to invent a public identity was caught and stopped. Identity is governed or discovered later in someone else's outbox.
- Grok Bot and Grok Automations are different products. Automations (launched 2026-07-16) are the lighter scheduler on grok.com and the Grok app. Grok Bot is the employee: skills, routines, shared computer, connectors. Team Bots on Teams and Enterprise as of 2026-09-28. Primary Bot as of 2026-10-01. Grok 4.7 is the model behind the current Bot harness.
- Eligibility as stated: paid individual Cursor or Cursor Teams, or individual SuperGrok / SuperGrok Plus / SuperGrok Heavy linked to the Cursor account. X Premium+ qualifies if that X account is linked. SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok Team, and SuperGrok Enterprise do not. The link cannot be unlinked or moved. Prices he cites for late September 2026: SuperGrok about $30/month, SuperGrok Plus about $100. Prices move.
- Bots on one account share the computer. Names are a visual boundary, not a security boundary. An instruction not to open finance guides behavior. It does not enforce it. Different trust means different accounts. Never paste a password into the chat. Authenticate inside the session.
- A skill is the method. A routine is the clock or event. Do the job by hand, make it reliable, save the method, only then schedule it. A Bot can own up to 50 routines. The app keeps the 20 most recent run records. Hiding a Bot does not pause its routines.
- Five fields settle later arguments. Owns. Inputs. May (search, read, summarize, classify, compare, organize, draft, stage). Must ask before (send, publish, purchase, delete, overwrite). Done when. Plus: when unsure, stop and ask.
- Trial before tools: walk through the plan, name every tool and every unsure judgment, stop and wait.
- Probation is three runs, not one. Autonomy levels 0–4: observe, prepare, execute with approval, run on a schedule, coordinate. Promote on evidence: five consecutive clean runs, verification passing, zero unresolved side effects, rollback tested once, approval policy actually tested. Demote if quality drops, a connector changes, or you are hand-correcting two weeks in a row.
- Second hire only when a bottleneck can be named. Pass a handoff, not a transcript.
- Three report surfaces: Grok Bot app (system of record), phone (one-line tap), email (filing cabinet, subject like =[BOT] Friday Scan 2026-10-04 PASS=). Body order: status, what changed, what it did not do, what needs you, links. Missing source is FAIL. Do not backfill from last week.
- Five watches before a fleet: receipt, heartbeat, source check, approval log, doubt line. If you would not notice the routine disappearing, delete it.
- Marketplace install copies instructions, boundaries, skills, and routines. It does not copy memory, files, or logins. Official shelf: https://x.ai/bot/marketplace . Community directories claim on the order of two thousand public listings. His own example Bot is News Honestly (https://x.ai/bot/vKZRklu07uF34Ut9XzMxt). * Ten-day plan

1. Write the one-sentence job and the five fields. Do not open the app until the sentence exists.
2. Create the Bot. Paste the description. Connect nothing.
3. Dry run in text. Correct the plan.
4. Connect one key. Run the boring job while you watch.
5. Second run, on a different but comparable input. Repair the rule.
6. Third run. Score it.
7. Save the method as a skill. Include the failure rule and the approval line.
8. Schedule it. Confirm timezone, input, expected result, and the down-source rule. Leave sends off.
9. Turn on the app alert and the filing email. Fixed subject line.
10. Friday receipt. Keep, fix, or fire. Only then consider a second hire.
