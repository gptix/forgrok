* Overview

This node is the entry point for a high-value collection of free, publicly available university courses and lecture series in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

The material was compiled by @shikamarutsai and published on X on 2026-08-16 (post ID 2088918066310652358). * Landmark Courses (Direct Links)

These are the highest-signal courses from the collection. Prefer these when starting deep work.

** Deep Learning

- *CS231n: Deep Learning for Computer Vision* (Stanford)  
  https://cs231n.github.io/

- *MIT 6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning* (Alexander Amini et al.)  
  https://introtodeeplearning.com/

- *NYU Deep Learning* (Yann LeCun & Alfredo Canziani)  
  https://atcold.github.io/NYU-DLSP20/

- *CS230: Deep Learning* (Andrew Ng, Stanford)  
  https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs230

** Natural Language Processing

- *CS224n: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning* (Stanford)  
  http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/

** Reinforcement Learning

- *David Silver – Reinforcement Learning* (UCL / DeepMind)  
  Classic lecture series (YouTube playlist starting with Lecture 1):  
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2pWv7GOvuf0  
  Slides historically at: http://www0.cs.ucl.ac.uk/staff/d.silver/web/Teaching.html (archived)

- *CS 285: Deep Reinforcement Learning* (Sergey Levine, UC Berkeley)  
  https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse
