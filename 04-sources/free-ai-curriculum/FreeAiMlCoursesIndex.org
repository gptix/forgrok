* Key Characteristics of the Collection

- Almost everything is free and publicly accessible
- Heavy emphasis on top-tier institutions (Stanford, MIT, CMU, Berkeley, Oxford, DeepMind, NYU, etc.)
- Many landmark courses (CS231n, CS224n, David Silver RL, Andrew Ng courses, Hinton, LeCun, Abbeel, Levine, etc.)
- Time span roughly 2011–2022
- Mix of full semester courses, summer schools, and focused lecture series
- Video lectures available for the large majority of entries
