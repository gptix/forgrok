* Dissent and a measured routing result
From the original, section =Summary= (comment links in the table):

- hyunsoo36 reported that in practice the compiled wiki often lacked the detail needed to answer a question, so the agent still had to read the raw sources. The wiki then became an extra layer whose retrieval savings did not offset its ingest cost.
- karlwirth argued that the wiki should be written for the human, not the agent. When several agents run at once, a richer wiki adds overhead rather than clarity; what is needed instead is a concise record of decisions, open questions, and status summaries.
- sassarini-marco reported that 72% of LLM tokens during ingest went to routing decisions (which folder, whether to merge, what to link). Offloading those decisions to a small typed classifier cut ingest time in half and raised retrieval success from 62% to 89% on a 525-document corpus.

* Data integrity
From the original, section =Summary=:

- NDOTO-G described hash-chain verification with health checks that flag when a wiki page silently diverges from its source.
- frankchu91 (MindBase 0.4.6) added computed, not LLM-guessed, lint checks for broken citations and broken wikilinks.
- francisdu53 replaced vector search with a Fuzzy Cognitive Map laid over the pages, reporting five weeks of daily production use. Project: https://github.com/francisdu53/exia-seed
