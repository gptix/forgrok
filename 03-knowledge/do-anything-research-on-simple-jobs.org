** 3. Online Flipping (eBay / Facebook Marketplace)

*** What It Is
Buy used items at low prices (thrift stores, garage sales, Facebook Marketplace) and resell them online at a markup. Focus on categories with high sell-through: electronics, brand-name clothing, tools, vintage items, collectibles.

*** Startup Costs (Itemized)

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------+------+-------|
| Initial inventory (3-5 items) | $50-100 | Start small |
| Shipping supplies | $15-30 | Poly mailers, tape, scale from Amazon |
| eBay fees (listing + final value) | ~15% of sales | Included in pricing; not upfront |
| PayPal/Stripe fees | ~3% | Included in pricing |
| Camera phone | $0 | Already have a smartphone |
| Cobb County license | $0-75 | Required if doing as a business |
| Total out-of-pocket | ~$65-175 | |

*** Time to First Dollar
~Week 1~. You can photograph items you already own and list them on Day 1. First sale within 3-7 days.

*** Realistic Monthly Income Range
- Month 1: ~$100-300/month (starting with items from around the house + small thrift runs).
- Month 2-3: ~$300-600/month (with reinvestment of profits into more inventory).
- Month 6+: ~$600-1,500/month (part-time, consistent sourcing routine).
[Sources: [[https://powersellingmom.com/how-much-money-can-you-really-make-reselling-on-ebay/][Power Selling Mom]], [[https://www.sharetown.com/blog/how-much-money-you-can-realistically-make-flipping-items-part-time][ShareTown]]]

*** Required Skills/Tools
- ~Eye for value.~ Knowing what sells. Start by researching "sold" listings on eBay for items you recognize.
- ~Smartphone camera.~ Good lighting and clear photos are 80% of the sale.
- ~Basic shipping.~ Print labels at home, drop at USPS. No trips needed beyond that.
- ~Time:~ ~30-40 minutes per item~ for sourcing, listing, packing, shipping.

*** How to Find First Customers (No Cold Calls)
- ~eBay / Facebook Marketplace:~ These are self-serve platforms. Customers find you via search.
- ~Local FB groups:~ "Vinings Buy Nothing" or "Marietta Yard Sales" for sourcing cheap/ free items.
- ~Tip:~ Start by selling items you already own (clothes, electronics, books). Zero cost and teaches the platform.

*** Georgia / Cobb County Licensing, Registration, Tax, Insurance
- ~Cobb County:~ Occupation Tax Certificate required if selling as a business (versus occasional casual sales).
- ~Georgia Sales Tax:~ Required if selling tangible goods in GA OR to GA residents. You need a Georgia Sales and Use Tax Registration from DOR [[https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/register-new-business-georgia][georgia.gov]]. Collect 8% (Cobb County rate) on in-state sales. Out-of-state sales via eBay may have eBay collecting marketplace facilitator tax.
- ~Trade name:~ DBA if using a business name.
- ~Insurance:~ Not needed for flipping at this scale.

*** Main Risks and Failure Modes
- ~Paying too much for inventory.~ Solution: Use eBay "sold items" filter to check actual sale prices before buying anything.
- ~Slow shipping / bad feedback.~ Solution: Ship within 24 hours. Use tracking always.
- ~Items don't sell.~ Solution: List on multiple platforms (eBay + FB Marketplace). Price at 90% of comps.
- ~Storage clutter.~ Solution: limit inventory to 10-15 items at a time. Sell before buying more.
- ~Sales tax complexity.~ Solution: eBay handles most tax collection automatically for their platform.

* Georgia / Cobb Legal and Tax Notes

** Business License (Occupation Tax Certificate)
- Required for ALL businesses operating in Cobb County, including home-based and part-time. [[https://www.cobbcounty.gov/community-development/business-license/obtain-business-license][Cobb County site]].
- Apply via email to businesslicense@cobbcounty.gov. They assess fee based on business type and projected gross receipts.
- Home occupation businesses are exempt from Certificate of Occupancy requirement but still need the tax cert.
- Home Occupation Permit may be required depending on zoning. Verify for Vinings / unincorporated Cobb. UNVERIFIED.

** Business Structure
- ~Sole Proprietorship:~ Simplest. No state registration. File personal taxes. Report income on Schedule C (GA Form 500).
- ~LLC:~ $110 filing fee with GA Secretary of State [[https://gmcc.georgia.gov/register-llc][georgia.gov]]. Not needed at this stage. Can convert later.
- ~Trade Name (DBA):~ If operating under any name other than "Jud Taylor," register at Cobb County Superior Court. Cost ~$10-25 UNVERIFIED.

** Sales Tax
- Georgia sales tax ~does NOT apply to services~ (professional consulting, copywriting, automation services). Only tangible personal property.
- ~If flipping goods:~ You must register for GA Sales Tax if selling to GA residents. Visit [[https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/register-new-business-georgia][GA DOR]]. Cobb County rate: ~8% (6% state + ~2% local). eBay collects and remits marketplace facilitator tax automatically for eBay sales -- confirm this reduces your burden. UNVERIFIED.

** State Income Tax
- Georgia flat rate: 5.39% (2026). Report business profit/loss on GA Form 500, Schedule 3.
- No separate business tax return for sole proprietors.
- Estimated quarterly payments required if expecting to owe >$500 in state tax.

** Insurance
- ~General Liability:~ $21-47/mo for consultants. [[https://comparesmallbusinessinsurance.com/blog/consultant-insurance-cost/][Source]].
- ~Professional Liability:~ $45-110/mo.
- For automation/copywriting services: low risk. Defer insurance until you have first client asking for it.
- For flipping: general liability optional at this scale.

** Zoning / Home Occupation
- Cobb County allows home occupations. Restrictions: no visible business signage, no customer traffic, no noise/odors. [[https://www.cobbcounty.gov/community-development/business-license/obtain-business-license][Source]].
- Automation and copywriting services are zero-footprint -- no zoning issue.
