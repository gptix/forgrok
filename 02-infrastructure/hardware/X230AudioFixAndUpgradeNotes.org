#+DESCRIPTION: Full resolution of post-reboot silence on Ubuntu 22.04 X230, confirmation of left-speaker hardware failure, and notes on low-cost T480 replacement.

* Problem
After a reboot the X230 (Ubuntu 22.04) had no sound. GNOME Settings → Sound showed a completely blank Output Device dropdown. System volume and Over-Amplification were fine.

* Diagnosis
- ALSA correctly detected the hardware: HDA Intel PCH + Realtek ALC269VC Analog.
- =pactl list short sinks= showed only the null sink.
- Default sink was the placeholder =@DEFAULT_SINK@=.
- Session manager in use was the deprecated =pipewire-media-session= (PipeWire 0.3.48).
- =pw-cli ls Device= and =pw-cli ls Node= showed only the camera and the null sink — no ALSA audio devices at all.
- Journal for media-session was silent (no probing errors, just start/stop messages).

* Resolution
1. Installed WirePlumber:
   #+begin_src bash
   sudo apt install wireplumber
   #+end_src
2. Removed leftover symlink and switched session managers:
   #+begin_src bash
   sudo rm -f /etc/systemd/user/pipewire-session-manager.service
   systemctl --user daemon-reload
   systemctl --user disable --now pipewire-media-session
   systemctl --user enable --now wireplumber
   systemctl --user restart pipewire pipewire-pulse
   #+end_src
3. Result: =alsa_output.pci-0000_00_1b.0.analog-stereo= appeared immediately. GNOME Settings now correctly shows “Speakers – Built-in Audio” / “Analog Stereo Output”.

* Current Audio State (2026-08-19)
- Software path is healthy.
- Right internal speaker works.
- Left internal speaker is dead (confirmed: headphones produce correct stereo; ALSA mixer shows both channels at 100 % and unmuted).
- Almost certainly a failed left speaker or its ribbon cable (common on aging X230s).
- Random instantaneous full shutdowns also occur; may be related to the same cable/ribbon area under the palmrest.

* Hardware Next Steps
- Replacement speaker set (genuine 04W1412 left+right) costs $10–17.
- Opening the machine to reseat the speaker cable + keyboard/palmrest ribbons is recommended (also may help the random shutdowns).

* Upgrade Interest
Considering a low-cost used replacement. Primary candidate is the ThinkPad T480:
- 8th-gen Intel, dual-battery hot-swap, dual storage, up to 32 GB RAM, still highly repairable.
- Current used prices (mid-2026): roughly $150–280 for solid i5/16 GB configurations.
- Natural next step from the X230 while keeping classic ThinkPad repairability and Linux friendliness.

Other cheap options noted: T470, X280, T490.
