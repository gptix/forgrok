#+DESCRIPTION: Diagnostic notes and parts options for intermittent driver-side low beam on 2013 Escape Titanium (VIN 1FMCU9J90DUA70448)

* Context
- Vehicle: 2013 Ford Escape Titanium AWD, 2.0L EcoBoost
- VIN: 1FMCU9J90DUA70448
- Symptom: Driver-side low-beam lamp works intermittently
- System: Factory HID (xenon), not halogen. Low beams use D3S bulbs + ballasts

* Diagnostic approach recommended
1. Perform a side-to-side bulb swap test first.
   - Move the known-good passenger D3S bulb to the driver side.
   - If the fault follows the bulb → replace the bulb.
   - If the fault stays on the driver side → suspect ballast, connector, wiring, or (less commonly) the Body Control Module.
2. While the headlight assembly is accessible, inspect the ballast connector and wiring for corrosion, heat damage, or loose pins.
3. Multimeter continuity / voltage checks on the ballast input side can be done once the assembly is out.

* Access notes
- Ballast mounts on the underside of the headlight assembly.
- Full access typically requires removing the front bumper / nose.
- Many owners replace both D3S bulbs and both ballasts while the nose is off, plus cornering / fog / marker bulbs.

* Parts information
** OEM ballast examples
- BL7Z-13C170-A
- DL3Z-13C170-A
- 8A5Z-13C170-A
- Dealer pricing commonly $350–$570+

** Aftermarket ballast options (approximate, mid-2026)
- Budget direct-fit units claiming compatibility with the above Ford numbers: $45–$80 each
- Established-brand aftermarket (Dorman, Standard Motor Products, etc.): $200–$320 each
- Ballast + D3S bulb kits marketed for the Escape: often $50–$90 for one side

Prefer units explicitly listed as replacements for 8A5Z-13C170-A / BL7Z-13C170-A / DL3Z-13C170-A.

* Practical recommendations
- Confirm with the bulb swap before buying a ballast.
- If a ballast is needed, the $50–$80 direct-fit range is the usual practical choice for most owners.
- Consider doing both sides while the bumper is off.
- Intermittent behavior on these cars has also been linked to BCM issues in some cases; the swap test helps isolate the fault.
