* Machine identity
| Field | Value |

| Model | ThinkPad T470s |

Chassis uses *two internal non-removable 3-cell packs*. Combined capacity about 50–51 Wh. Shapes and connectors differ; packs are *not* interchangeable.

* Batteries
| Location | iFixit name | Role | Capacity / voltage | Official FRUs (substitutes) | Common ASM / model |
|----------|-------------|------|--------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------|
| Front (under trackpad) | Battery 1 | Larger pack | 24 Wh, 11.25–11.4 V, 3-cell Li-ion | 00HW022 (Panasonic), 00HW023 (SMP/Simplo) | SB10F46460, SB10F46461 (later lists also 00HW036 / 00HW037) |
| Rear (near display hinge) | Battery 2 | Smaller pack | 26 Wh (later 26.1 Wh), 11.4 V, 3-cell Li-ion | 01AV405 / 01AV406, later 01AV462 | L16M3P73, SB10J79002, SB10J79003, 01AV407 / 01AV408 |

- Front slot only: 00HW022 or 00HW023 (and listed later equivalents).
- Rear slot only: 01AV405, 01AV406, or 01AV462 (and listed later equivalents).
- Do not put a 24 Wh front pack in the rear bay or a 26 Wh rear pack in the front bay.
- Replacing only one pack is electrically possible; remaining aged pack still drags runtime and charge behavior. Usual service advice: replace both together.
- Aftermarket listings often say "T470s 20HF / 20HF0066US". Match *shape of the pack you pull*, not only the model string.

**Buy list for this MT:** one front pack from the 00HW022/00HW023 family *and* one rear pack from the 01AV405/01AV406/01AV462 family.

* Speakers
| Field | Value |
|-------+-------|
| Official FRU | 00JT988 |
| Also marked on drivers | PK23000N2Y0 / PK23000N2N0 |
| Form | One assembly, left + right together |
| Electrical | 2 W / 4 Ω thin-chassis |
| Shared with | T460s 20F9 / 20FA |
| Does *not* fit | Regular T460 or T470 (avoid 01AV902 / "T470 speakers") |
| HMM procedure | 1120 |
| Fasteners | Six bronze M2 × 5 mm screws |
| Connector | 4-pin, 4-wire |

Access is bottom cover + both internal batteries first. Route the cable as in the HMM or it pinches under a battery.

Purchase order:
1. Genuine 00JT988 at a sane authorized-parts price.
2. New aftermarket that prints *both* 00JT988 and PK23000N2Y0, with photos of the actual pair and 4-pin plug.
3. Avoid T470/T470p speaker SKUs.

Do batteries and speakers in the same sitting.

* Service notes (do not treat as a how-to beyond the HMM)
- Disable built-in batteries in BIOS before opening the bottom cover.
- Phillips #000.
- iFixit / HMM alignment in the source chat: 5 bottom-cover screws, then 2 on the front pack and 3 on the rear pack.
- Lenovo parts lookup still lists genuine FRUs if the full serial is entered.
