#+DESCRIPTION: Standing constraint on retire-and-replace: a role can be handed to a fresh bot from a one-page note, but no bot is replaced, swapped mid-job, or deleted unless George says so. The old bot is renamed (retired) rather than deleted.

* Rule
1. Each role (job, report line, rules) should be kept so a fresh bot can take the same role from a one-page handover note: pointer to the role, standing rules, live state, open work, pending decisions, routines to recreate, and lessons, with no secrets.
2. No bot is replaced, swapped mid-job, or deleted unless George says so. If a bot is retired, it is renamed "(retired)" and is not deleted without his OK.
3. A usage-by-bot check and a handover draft may be prepared as a proposal only. Preparing the draft is not permission to create, rename, or retire any bot.
