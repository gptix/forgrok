* Rule (adopted 2026-11-16; dated 2026-11-13 team review)
Every bot on the team:
1. Drafts only. It sends, buys, or signs up for nothing without George's OK.
2. Keeps private material in the private repository, never in this one.
3. Commits or pushes only when George says push (see [[id:0d4bb96a-d26a-459c-8f80-d2ff513ea225][ApprovalBeforeCommit]] and [[id:747f93df-39a4-4c48-8699-9496888623cc][GitAuthorPerBot]]).
4. Tells Gregorio only about matters that cross areas. Single-area work goes straight between George and the specialist (the 2026-11-13 routing rule, proposed as DirectToSpecialistRouting).

New project bots follow the same pattern: a short project brief plus this rule.

* Exception kept on purpose
George's ProtonMail forwards to the team inbox are still routed by Gregorio to the right specialist, who then works with George directly.

* Two Tsuioku workflows, each labeled
- *Workflow A, George's own nodes:* a bot drafts node text in the GenerateTsuiokuNodeText format; George dry-runs with =C-u C-c n F=, commits with =C-c n F= (no push), and pushes with Magit.
- *Workflow B, bot-made private batches* (for example Read Multiplex and Roemmele X summaries): after George says push, the bot adds the =:ID:= drawers, commits under its own "(for gptix)" author, and pushes to the private repository; George pulls on the T470s and runs =org-roam-db-sync=.
