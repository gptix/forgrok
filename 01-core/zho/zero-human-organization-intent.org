* Summary

Intent to establish one or more Zero-Human Organizations (ZHOs) modeled on Brian Roemmele’s Zero-Human Company concept. Day-to-day operations are to be driven by autonomous AI agents with minimal ongoing human staffing. Three explicit mission pillars have been defined.

* Three Mission Pillars

** 1. High-Protein Content (HPC) Collection
[[id:b622af12-faaa-4b6f-95bc-aa909d0cfc8f][HighProteinContent]]
- Prioritize dense, high-signal material over high-volume low-density internet content.
- Examples: older technical literature, primary historical documents, court opinions, engineering handbooks, careful empirical work, pre-internet sources.
- Aligns directly with Roemmele’s high-protein vs low-protein distinction.
- Existing local AI + org-roam pipelines are already well-suited; agents handle discovery, filtering, chunking, and embedding under human-defined inclusion criteria.

** 2. “Save-Your-Wisdom” Strategy
[[id:b72a8d1b-04b8-4783-9c56-96b9a17f57f3][SaveYourWisdom]]
- Enable and politely promote practical tools and workflows that help individuals capture personal knowledge, family history, professional judgment, and hard-won experience.
- Framing remains non-coercive and service-oriented.
- Agents can generate personalized capture templates, conduct guided interviews, and produce clean structured outputs.

** 3. Automated Public Arrestee Data Pipeline
[[id:fdfd59a0-a2e1-4eea-aefc-983544ebaa90][PublicArresteeData]]
- Scope limited strictly to data that is already publicly available under open-records laws.
- Geographic focus: Georgia (especially Fulton County and related city police/court systems) under the Georgia Open Records Act.
- Reference model: work similar in spirit to Matt Van Swol’s public-records efforts.
- Explicit concern: political or ideological beliefs of individual academics may interfere with neutral methodology. Therefore prefer primary-source and practitioner-driven approaches.

* Legal & Operational Guardrails (Arrestee Data)

- Only collect and republish what is already public.
- Respect published access methods, rate limits, robots.txt, and formal request channels.
- Recommended bias-resistant process:
  1. Inventory every relevant agency and its current public-access methods.
  2. Use agents to generate and track formal Open Records requests where portals are incomplete.
  3. Cross-check names, dates, charges, and dispositions against multiple public sources.
  4. Store raw public data + full provenance (request, response date, agency) for auditability.
  5. Release only what is already public, with clear documentation of sources and known gaps.
- Preferred advisors: experienced open-records officers, data journalists with multi-jurisdiction experience, FOIA/open-records specialists, and public-interest technologists who treat primary documents as ground truth.

* Architectural Fit

- Builds directly on existing Hermes Agent / Withnail local-first, largely air-gapped infrastructure.
- Suggested agent roles:
  - Source discovery & formal request drafting
  - Ingestion & normalization of public records
  - Quality / completeness / provenance checking
  - HPC filtering and knowledge-graph integration
  - Public-facing release pipelines
- Human layer remains thin: purpose-setting, quality criteria, legal guardrails, and occasional high-stakes decisions.

* Open Next Steps (from discussion)

- Sketch concrete agent role definitions and skill/tool boundaries for the arrestee-data pipeline.
- Produce templates for Georgia Open Records requests.
- Structure a jurisdiction inventory (starting with Fulton County and surrounding cities).
