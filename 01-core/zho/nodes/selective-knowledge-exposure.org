* Definition

Selective Knowledge Exposure is the deliberate practice of giving the autonomous CEO agent (Withnail / Hermes) access only to a carefully chosen subset of the full knowledge base.

Instead of mounting the entire ~/local-side/ (or the full forgrok repository), a restricted view — typically ~/withnail-kb/ or an equivalent curated export — is provided to the agent.

This is a core operational complement to the Air-gapped / Near-Airgapped CEO Pattern.

* Design Intent

- Reduce the chance of the agent acting on stale, sensitive, or out-of-scope material.
- Keep the agent’s context window and reasoning focused on high-value, current, and approved knowledge.
- Maintain a clear boundary between the full operator knowledge base and what the autonomous executive is allowed to see and reason over.
- Support the long-term goal of a trustworthy, mostly self-contained local CEO.

* Current Practice (as of 2026-08)

- Primary restricted view: ~/withnail-kb/ (or equivalent selective export)
- Full knowledge base remains on the operator side (X230 + GitHub gptix/forgrok)
- Changes to what Withnail can see are intentional and reviewed
