* Definition

Save-Your-Wisdom is the second explicit mission pillar of the Zero-Human Organization Intent.

It is the deliberate effort to enable and politely promote practical tools and workflows that help individuals capture personal knowledge, family history, professional judgment, and hard-won experience before it is lost.

The framing remains non-coercive and service-oriented.

* Core idea

Most high-value human knowledge lives in people’s heads and private notes. It is rarely written down in durable, structured, or shareable form. Save-Your-Wisdom treats the capture and structuring of that knowledge as a first-class activity — one that autonomous agents can assist with at scale while remaining under human direction.

* Why it matters

- Complements High-Protein Content (public / institutional knowledge) with private / personal knowledge.
- Creates long-term compounding value for individuals and families.
- Provides a natural domain for local-first, privacy-respecting agents (Withnail / Hermes).
- Aligns with the broader Roemmele-inspired emphasis on preserving what is actually worth keeping.

* Current practice and assets (as of 2026-08)

- Related material exists under =04-sources/savewisdom/=.
- Earlier work on the “1000 Save Wisdom Questions” has been ingested into the knowledge base.
- Agents can already generate personalized capture templates and structured outputs; this pillar formalizes the intent.

* Relationship to the other two pillars

- **HighProteinContent**: Public and institutional high-signal material.
- **PublicArresteeData**: Public records that are already legally available.
- **SaveYourWisdom**: Personal and professional knowledge that only the individual possesses.

Together the three pillars cover institutional, public-record, and personal knowledge.
