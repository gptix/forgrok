* Definition

Public Arrestee Data is the third explicit mission pillar of the Zero-Human Organization Intent.

It refers to the systematic collection, normalization, provenance-tracking, and (where appropriate) republication of arrest and related court records that are already publicly available under open-records law — primarily the Georgia Open Records Act — with a geographic focus on Fulton County and surrounding jurisdictions.

* Scope and hard limits

- Only data that is already public.
- No private, sealed, juvenile, or non-public records.
- Strict respect for published access methods, rate limits, robots.txt, and formal request channels.
- Full provenance required (agency, request/response dates, source URL or request ID).

* Why it matters

- Primary public records are high-protein source material.
- Creates a durable, auditable, bias-resistant dataset for later analysis.
- Demonstrates a concrete Zero-Human Organization capability (agent-driven discovery, formal requests, ingestion, and quality control).
- Aligns with the broader goal of recovering and preserving high-value public information that is otherwise fragmented or ephemeral.

* Current practice and assets (as of 2026-08)

- Georgia Open Records Request Template exists.
- Jurisdiction inventory work has begun (Fulton County + related cities).
- Explicit methodological preference for primary-source / practitioner-driven approaches over academic intermediaries.
- Intended to feed both the knowledge graph and the selective knowledge exposure slice available to Withnail.

* Relationship to the other two pillars

- **HighProteinContent**: Public arrest and court records, when properly provenance-tracked, are treated as high-protein material.
- **SaveYourWisdom**: Complementary — one pillar preserves institutional/public memory; the other helps individuals preserve personal and professional knowledge.
