* Message of the series
Five recommendations:
1. Use Grok Bot for teams you can build, email via mail.grokbot.com, telephony, routines, connectors.
2. Use Emacs and org-roam to maintain knowledge bases.
3. Use local machines with local models for routine work.
4. Use Omarchy.
5. Use Emacs on Omarchy to write specialized Elisp functions.
