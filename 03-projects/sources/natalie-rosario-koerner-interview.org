* Key Takeaways (Summary)

Natalie operates as a prime contractor with essentially no operational staff. She:

- Wins contracts in domains she has no prior experience in (hazardous waste, landscaping, catering, etc.)
- Subcontracts 100% of the performance work
- Uses Claude (preferred) and ChatGPT to analyze long solicitations and draft responses
- Uses USAspending.gov for historical pricing to set markups
- Reports roughly one hour per month of ongoing work once contracts are active
- First contract she bid on was the first she won
- Emphasizes generic LLC naming and free SAM.gov registration as the critical starting points
