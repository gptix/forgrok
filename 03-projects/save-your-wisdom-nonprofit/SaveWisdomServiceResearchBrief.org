* Summary of findings
1. SaveWisdom (Brian Roemmele, launched 2023-06-24) is a free, open method, not a paid service. A person records spoken answers to up to 1,000 questions (v7.0, 2025-02-08) on an OFFLINE voice recorder. The audio is transcribed locally (iFLYTEK offline or Whisper), and a private "Wisdom Keeper" AI comes later. No public toolkit for the AI step has shipped, and ReadMultiplex forum members were still asking about its status.
2. *Licensing constraint.* The question PDF says the questions "can not be used in whole or in part in a publication or list that is in whole or part of a commercial work that is being sold directly or as a subscription." A paid service needs its own question set, Brian's written permission, or a design in which the client uses the free list directly.
3. Tsuioku already holds the core material: the 1,000 questions (PDF, XLSX, TXT and a cleaned org node), the forum thread, the Save-Your-Wisdom ZHO pillar node (2026-08-07) and the Librarian Bot plan adapted from Roemmele (2026-10-26).
4. Lessons Learned programs (CALL, MCCLL, NLLIS, JLLP/JLLIS, NASA LLIS, DOE) supply the rigor that SaveWisdom lacks: observation, then insight, then validated lesson, then dissemination and evaluation. They capture institutional, actionable knowledge. SaveWisdom captures personal, tacit, emotional knowledge. The Army is now putting AI on top of its lessons (Quick-Fire/Vantage, and VICTOR in Aug 2026).
5. *Market.* Consumer services are cheap and cloud-hosted: StoryWorth from $59, Remento $99/yr, StoryCorps free. The AI-persona category is unstable. A competitor reports that HereAfter AI posted a shutdown notice in July 2026, and eternos.life now redirects to Uare.ai. Legacy video costs about $1,750 to $8,000 (Telloom). Enterprise expert capture is a separate, pricier niche.
6. *The opening for George.* A local-first, client-owned, validated service: offline recorder, local transcription, org-roam or plain-text output that the family keeps, and a lessons-learned-style validation pass. This is the reverse of the subscription lock-in that failed HereAfter customers.

* 3. SaveWisdom: what, how, status, tech
Sources: [[https://savewisdom.org/][SaveWisdom.org]], [[https://savewisdom.org/the-1000-word-save-wisdom-questions/][The 1000 Save Wisdom Questions]], [[https://savewisdom.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/SaveWisdomQuestions-NEW-1.pdf][1000 Questions PDF v7.0]].

| Aspect | Finding (savewisdom.org) |
|--------+--------------------------|
| What | A mission to preserve every person's wisdom "for you, your family or the world." With permission, it aims at a Teilhard de Chardin "Noosphere". |
| Method | Up to 1,000 questions in categories (Category 1: Childhood, Family & Parenting, Q1-100, and so on). Record alone, for example in a car or a closet. State the question, pause, then answer at length. Emotion counts as data. |
| Hardware | Any offline voice memo device ("many are below $50"). The site favors an iFLYTEK fully offline recorder with transcription (about $200 on sale, per the site) and urges never storing recordings on a cloud-linked device. Device links are Amazon affiliate links. |
| AI step | SD-card audio goes to local speech-to-text, which feeds a private local model. A voice model is optional. Step-by-step "forthcoming articles" are promised. No public toolkit found. [status: not shipped, as far as public sources show] |
| License | Free for personal use. Not allowed in commercial works sold directly or by subscription. |
| Collaborators | OSV (osv.llc) reported a collaboration in its "Wisdom Unleashed" newsletter. Its blog page now returns 404. |

* 4. Lessons Learned programs
| Program | Essentials |
|---------+------------|
| Center for Army Lessons Learned (CALL) | Uses the OIL model: Observations (conditions), Insights (issues), Lessons (solutions). OIL "do not constitute lessons learned" until they change behavior or DOTMLPF. Collection is active (embedded teams, AARs) and passive (submissions). The Quick-Fire/Vantage platform adds generative-AI search and summaries. 2025 guidance on CamoGPT and NIPR GPT stresses SME validation. |
| Marine Corps Center for Lessons Learned (MCCLL) / MCLLS | Governed by MCO 3504.1. Runs an annual Collection Campaign Plan (FY25: MARADMIN 424/24) with embedded analysts, and each collection ends in a final report. The 1990s MCLLS software was the baseline for JLLIS. |
| Navy Lessons Learned Information System (NLLIS) | The Navy's instance of JLLIS, in classified and unclassified versions. CAC holders self-register. |
| Joint Lessons Learned Program (JLLP) / JLLIS | Five phases: discovery, validation, resolution, evaluation, dissemination. Governed by CJCSI 3150.25H (2021, Change 1 2024) and CJCSM 3150.25C (2023). JLLIS is the shared system across the services. [Air Force-specific details not verified] |
| NASA LLIS + APPEL Knowledge Management | An agency-wide reviewed repository (collect, record, disseminate, apply). The NASA OIG (IG-12-012) found it underused. A 2025 Knowledge Continuity guide covers departing experts: interviews, video capture, shadowing and continuity books. |
| DOE Lessons Learned (DOE-HDBK-7502-95) | Site-level programs linked into DOE-wide electronic sharing, with lessons fed into work planning. |
| CDC | No specific CDC program page was verified this session (cdc.gov blocked automated checks). [unverified] |
| AI era: Army VICTOR (2026-08-05) | A mobile-first AI platform from Combined Arms Command that connects doctrine and lessons-learned silos into synthesized answers. An initial cohort began in Aug 2026, with a force-wide launch targeted for fall 2026. |

** Similarities and differences with personal wisdom capture
| Dimension | Lessons Learned | SaveWisdom-style | What to borrow |
|-----------+-----------------+------------------+----------------|
| Unit of capture | An observation tied to an event (AAR) | An answer to a life question | Tag each answer as an observation with date, context and narrator |
| Tacit knowledge | Embedded collectors interview people | The narrator talks alone, unprompted | Add an optional interviewer follow-up pass for experts |
| Validation | SMEs validate before anything becomes a lesson | None. The narrator's truth is the point. | Verify facts (dates, names), keep opinions verbatim, and have the narrator review the transcript (OHA practice) |
| Purpose | Change doctrine and training | Legacy, family, personal AI | An "expert edition" for retiring professionals that produces actionable lessons |
| Dissemination | Controlled, often classified | Private, then family, then public only with consent | Tiered release with executor rules, mirroring Tsuioku's public/private split |
| Known failure | Lessons observed but not learned (NASA OIG) | Recordings never transcribed, toolkit never shipped | Close the loop: every session yields an org node plus a reviewed summary |

*Positioning gap:* no service found combines offline capture, client-owned plain-text or org output, structured validation and an optional local AI. That combination is the SaveWisdom vision, and nobody has built the paid toolkit for it.

* 6. Recommended Skills and Agents
Influences:
- Roemmele's "hire, don't build" and his Librarian Bot ([[https://x.ai/bot/HKZR4k_DCSzy2PSnkkE8v][template]]).
- poteto's [[https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/README.md][pstack]], which favors skills over sprawl: /interrogate, /reflect, /correct, /recall.
- xAI's Grok Bot templates.

Rule of thumb: create a new bot only for a distinct job with its own permissions and data. Otherwise write a skill.

** Skills
These are cheap, versioned in 08-skills, and usable by any bot.

| Skill | What it does | Pros | Cons |
|-------+--------------+------+------|
| wisdom-question-set | Maintains George's own license-clean question bank, with expert-edition variants. Records which SaveWisdom question inspired each item, without copying it. | Solves the license problem and can be reused | Real writing effort. Must avoid near-copies. |
| wisdom-session-intake | Takes an SD-card dump, runs local Whisper on the T470s and writes one org-roam node per answer (question ID, date, narrator, audio link, verbatim text) | Local-first and Emacs-native. Extends tsuioku-file-intake. | Whisper is slow on the T470s CPU. Keep audio out of git (git-annex or external storage). |
| oil-validation-pass | Separates facts to verify from opinions kept verbatim. Extracts observation, insight and lesson for the expert edition. Queues narrator review. | The rigor that differentiates the service | Can sanitize the narrator's voice, so keep it opt-in |
| consent-and-release | Consent form, release tiers (private, family, public) and an executor clause, modeled on OHA and StoryCorps | Builds trust | Needs legal review. This brief is not legal advice. |
| wisdom-keeper-build | Packages the corpus for local RAG (Withnail/hermes-pi). Voice cloning only with documented consent. | Delivers the Wisdom Keeper that Brian promised | Voice-clone fraud risk (FraudFighter angle). Fast-moving tech. |
| export-and-exit | Always hands the client audio, transcripts and org/markdown/PDF on a USB drive | The direct lesson from HereAfter | No lock-in revenue. That is the point. |

** Agents (bots)
| Option | Role | Pros | Cons |
|--------+------+------+------|
| Lisbet Librarian (existing) plus the skills | Files wisdom nodes and runs the weekly Librarian audit | No new hire. Already scoped to Tsuioku. | Mixes client corpora with George's KB unless the repos are separate |
| NEW Wisdom Interviewer bot | Sends weekly prompts, asks follow-ups and logs sessions | Matches what Remento and StoryWorth do. Own scoped permissions. | A cloud bot works against the offline ethos. Elders need a very simple channel, such as phone or SMS. |
| NEW Wisdom Archivist (local, on Withnail) | Offline transcription, indexing and RAG on hermes-pi | Truest to SaveWisdom and George's local-first values | Hardware limits. More maintenance. |
| Roberto the Record Ranger (existing) | Organizes the photos and documents that go with recordings | Already fits | Scope creep |
| Gregorio (existing) | Market research, pricing and launch | A generalist already doing this work | None significant |
| FraudFighter (existing) | Guards against voice-clone and elder-fraud risks and reviews consent | Natural fit for an elder audience | Needs a clear scope |
| Librarian Bot (Roemmele template) | Audits standing rules across the bot team | Already planned in TsuiokuLibrarianBotPlan | Whether one bot can read the others' transcripts is unverified |

** Recommendation
1. Start with skills only.
2. Pilot on George first, then on one elder. Use wisdom-question-set, wisdom-session-intake and export-and-exit, run by Lisbet and Gregorio.
3. Hire a dedicated Wisdom Interviewer bot only when there is a second client.
4. Keep client corpora in separate private repos, never in forgrok.
5. Before charging anyone, ask Brian about licensing or collaboration through ReadMultiplex once the subscription is restored.
