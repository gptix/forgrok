* Project state as reviewed

Frozen current-state node is still dated 2026-08-08. STATUS.md (2026-08-31) still lists OpenRouter credits as the blocker for a paid-vision retest.

What the live Space actually does:

- Static Hugging Face Space (=sdk: static=)
- User supplies an OpenRouter key (localStorage)
- Upload a photo of physical cards
- Model ="openrouter/free"=
- Output is *identification only* (Rider-Waite names + upright/reversed when detectable)
- No question field, no structured JSON, no layout parser, no stored history, no full reading

Proven identification from a real photo: Ace of Swords, Wheel of Fortune, King of Wands.

Known limits from later pulses (2026-08-10 through 2026-08-16):

- 51 real-world test photographs, 33 scenario categories
- Single-card and medium spreads (~7 cards) are the reliable range
- Celtic Cross often returns an empty response on free models
- Batch script exists at ~/bin/tarot-batch-test.py on the X230
- Image-quality problems already catalogued: occlusion, tilt, glare/sleeves, mixed decks, clutter, color cast, low resolution, multiple spreads in one frame

Long-term product (unchanged): photograph a physical spread → identify cards + orientation + layout → coherent written interpretation. Web first (PWA already works in phone browsers), then store apps. Local-first / on-device vision remains the longer alignment.

* How the market actually splits

Two different products share the phrase "tarot reading app." They barely overlap.

1. *Photo of the user's physical spread* → identify → interpret.
   This is Tarot Today's mechanic. Several 2025–2026 apps claim it. None of them is a category-scale success yet.
2. *Digital draw inside the app or site* (RNG or a daily hand-shuffled order) → canned or AI prose.
   This is what made Labyrinthos, Trusted Tarot, Biddy Tarot, Galaxy Tarot, and similar properties successful.

Accurate one-line finding from the follow-up: image-of-your-spread *does* exist. It is not what built the large incumbents.

* Tier 2 — successful digital-draw services (not image upload)

These are the properties people already use. They do not identify a photo of an arbitrary physical spread.

**Learning / journal**

- Labyrinthos — https://labyrinthos.co/ plus iOS and Android. Category leader for learning. Android 1M+ downloads, ~4.7★ / 40k+ reviews; iOS ~4.9★ / 22k ratings. Lessons, quizzes, journals, multiple decks, optional AI-reading credits. Some users log *physical* draws in the journal by hand. Tina Gong / Labyrinthos LLC.
- Golden Thread Tarot — sister app from the same shop. Minimal, free, no ads. Aesthetic journal.
- Biddy Tarot — https://biddytarot.com/. Education business (Brigit Esselmont). Card-meaning encyclopedia that ranks for many card-name searches; courses and certification. Claims on the order of 15M annual visitors. Readings exist; the asset is the school and library.

**Free or cheap instant draws**

- Trusted Tarot — https://www.trustedtarot.com/. Long-running free web reader. Founder shuffles a physical deck daily (not a per-user RNG). On the order of 1.4M visits in Jan 2026. Site claims ~4.9★ / 66k+ reviews. Also has iOS/Android.
- Galaxy Tarot — Android staple. Historically huge review count. Later ads/subscription backlash. Still a reference app many readers keep.
- Tarot (Vázquez Software) — Android volume play. ~5.5M downloads, ~4.8★ / 65k reviews. Simple daily draws plus ads.

**Mass-market / AI-native 2025–2026**

- Tarot.com (Keen / Astrology.com family) — high traffic, astrology bundled, paid deep readings.
- Taro's Tarot — https://tarostarot.com/. AI web reader that writes to the question.
- CHATAROT.AI — https://chatarot.ai/en/ai-tarot-reading. 3D table, multiple AI "readers," follow-up chat.
- Tarotap, Overchat AI, Nummi, Raka, Sanctuary — subscription AI readers. Treat "best of 2026" listicles as marketing. Shared pattern: question field + adaptive prose + monthly fee.

**Live human marketplaces (adjacent search intent, different business)**

- Keen, Kasamba, Purple Garden, California Psychics. Per-minute chat / phone / video. This is where a lot of "I want a tarot reading" traffic lands. Price ceiling and App Store review risk ("fortune-telling"), not a product twin.

* Implications for Tarot Today

The open slot is still: *I already shuffled my own deck on the table.* Labyrinthos, Trusted Tarot, and Biddy do not fill it. The 2025–2026 scan apps are trying to, but they are small, deck-locked, or unproven.

Differentiation if identification becomes reliable:

1. Physical cards stay primary. The ritual is the product, not a virtual shuffle.
2. Vision → structured card list → interpretation. Not RNG dressed as mystery.
3. Later local-first / on-device or self-hosted vision. None of the named incumbents offer that.

Gaps already visible in successful apps, matching the 2026-08-16 entrepreneurial review:

- Optional question / intention field. Every AI reader that feels "personal" has one.
- Journal of *this physical spread* (Labyrinthos already lets people log manual draws).
- Explicit "not fortune-telling / reflection journal" framing for store review.
- Fail with a *specific* reason when the photo is bad (glare, crop, mixed deck, tilt). The iCards app shows what happens when scan fails silently.

Pricing context from the 2026-08-16 node is still the working band: subscription about $7.99–$9.99/mo or ~$49.99/yr; lifetime around $59.99; free tier useful enough to form a habit.

* Suggested next actions (unchanged in substance)

1. OpenRouter credits → re-test identification with a stronger vision model (Gemini 2.5 Flash was the preferred candidate on 2026-08-16).
2. Harden image-quality + orientation detection before adding more reading prose.
3. Add a short reading layer (2–3 lengths, one alternate voice) only after identification is stable.
4. Add optional question field and local save of valid spreads.
5. Keep watching Lantern Arcana, Tarot Line, and Taroter; use Labyrinthos (journal + learn) and Trusted Tarot (low-friction reading) as UX references, not as mechanic copies.
