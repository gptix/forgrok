Add-ons (do not bury in the base price):

- Design already in. Do not sell "undsigned piles."
- Extra hay bale $35 (Herrington lists straw $8 wholesale / min 10; retail hay in Atlanta is usually $12–20).
- Haul-away $95 (Spillers treats this as optional; ~90% of Heather's customers bought pickup).
- Stairs / no-driveway surcharge $40 if the crew cannot roll a cart to the stoop.

Cap custom at $2,500+ or refuse it in season one.

* Sell first, buy second

This is the operating rule.

1. Open a prepaid list *this week*. Square Invoice or Stripe Payment Link. No "hold my spot."
2. Take *100%* before a date is reserved (Heather year one). If that feels stiff for Buckhead, take 60% now and 40% seven days before the install week — but do not buy fruit on the unpaid 40%.
3. Hard cap season one at *36 paid installs* (six Saturday routes × six stops). Waitlist after 36.
4. Do not place a farm or Vienna order until *12 paid packages* are in the account (enough to justify one mixed bin + one white/heirloom pull).
5. Scout buy only: one Forest Park or Jaemor run after the first 5 paid jobs, to photograph real fruit for the listing. That is marketing inventory, not the season buy.

If paid demand dies at 8 houses, you never touch wholesale. That is success of the rule, not failure.

* Labor and setup

- Two people, 25–40 minutes on a Stoop, 45–70 on a Portico.
- Heather's 10-minute design is DFW muscle memory. Budget 20 minutes in season one.
- Buckhead constraints: brick stairs, no hay on some stoops, HOA sightlines, narrow streets, no overnight trailer. Photograph the porch from the prepaid form (two phone pics) so the crew is not designing cold.
- Customer need not be home (Spillers rule). Put that in the invoice.

Insurance: general liability before the first drop. Do not skip this in Buckhead.

* How this is not Praise / Spillers

You will not beat them on Instagram this month. Differentiating moves that cost little:

- Secular, design-led (no faith kit unless asked).
- Ghost package as a *named* non-orange SKU, not a custom.
- Prepaid weeks published (Spillers already does weeks; copy the mechanic).
- Smaller book, faster replies, same-week photo proof after install.

Do not race $375. Race *looks like $595 at $395* by keeping minis and two whites on the Stoop.
