- Packages and piece ranges from BuckheadPorchPumpkinPlan2026.

- Mid-count used whenever a bin lists a range (example: 40–45 ct → 42.5).
- =Each= means a printed 1-ea price, or bin price ÷ mid-count when no each exists.
- =Bin avg= always means bin price ÷ mid-count.
