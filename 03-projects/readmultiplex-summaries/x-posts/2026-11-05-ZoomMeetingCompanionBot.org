participant that listens, records structure, and responds only in text—"far more

for later questions without reopening the recording. Once calendar-connected, it
can pull Zoom URLs itself for recurring or one-off meetings. He stresses that the
bot cannot speak yet, so spoken answers still fall to him, and claims meeting-flow
context helps the bot guide him better afterward.
