* 3. Term
This is a month-to-month tenancy commencing on 14 August 2026 (“Start Date”) and continuing until terminated by either party in accordance with California law and this Agreement.

* 4. Rent
Monthly rent is $800.00, due in advance on the 1st day of each calendar month.  

Accepted payment methods:  
- Bank transfer to Landlord’s Bank of America checking account (preferred), or  
- Zelle to the account/email designated in writing by Landlord, or  
- Check made payable to “George P. Taylor IX” with memo “72122 Sunnyvale Rent”.

Rent is considered paid only when actually received by Landlord.  

If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $40.00 (five percent of the monthly rent). This amount is intended as a reasonable estimate of the administrative and other costs incurred by Landlord as a result of late payment, consistent with Civil Code § 1671.

* 5. Security Deposit
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $800.00 (one month’s rent) upon signing.  
The deposit is held as security for performance of Tenant’s obligations. Landlord may apply it to unpaid rent, cleaning beyond ordinary wear and tear, and repair of damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, in accordance with Civil Code § 1950.5.  
Within 21 calendar days after Tenant vacates and returns all keys, Landlord shall return any remaining balance together with an itemized statement of deductions.

* 6. Occupancy and Guests
The Premises may be occupied only by Tenant. No cohabitation or additional permanent occupants are permitted without Landlord’s prior written consent.  
Tenant may have at most two overnight guests at any time. No guest may stay more than seven (7) consecutive days (or more than fourteen (14) days in any calendar month) without Landlord’s prior written consent.  

Tenant agrees not to permit entry or visits by the specific individuals associated with the nearby residence (currently understood to be in the 72155–72165 Sunnyvale Drive area) of whom Tenant has actual knowledge and has been informed by Landlord, without Landlord’s prior written consent.

* 7. Pets
No animals or pets of any kind are permitted on the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent. Any consent may be conditioned on additional deposit, rent, or rules.  
(Status of any existing pets still to be confirmed.)

* 8. Smoking / Vaping
Smoking and vaping are permitted on the Premises.

* 9. Utilities and Services
Tenant is solely responsible for all utilities and services serving the Premises, including but not limited to water, gas, electricity, trash/recycling, sewer (if separately metered or billed), internet, and any other services. Tenant shall place all accounts in Tenant’s name as of the Start Date and keep them current.

* 10. Use, Care, and Maintenance
Tenant shall use the Premises only as a private residence and shall keep the Premises (including yard, if any) clean, sanitary, and in good functional order and repair. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repairs or conditions that may affect habitability or safety.  
Tenant is responsible for damage caused by Tenant, guests, or invitees beyond ordinary wear and tear. Landlord retains responsibility for major structural elements, roof, foundation, and systems necessary for habitability, subject to Tenant’s duty to report.

Tenant shall not make alterations, additions, or improvements without Landlord’s prior written consent.

* 11. Entry by Landlord
Landlord or Landlord’s agents may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, improvements, to show to prospective tenants or purchasers, or other lawful purposes, upon reasonable notice (normally 24 hours) and at reasonable times, consistent with Civil Code § 1954. Emergency entry is permitted without notice.

* 12. Assignment and Subletting
Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent. Any attempted assignment or subletting without consent is void and is a material breach.

* 13. Termination
Either party may terminate this month-to-month tenancy by giving written notice as required by Civil Code § 1946.1 (generally 30 days if tenancy has lasted less than one year; 60 days if one year or more). Landlord may also terminate for cause (nonpayment of rent, material breach after notice to cure, nuisance, illegal use, etc.) in accordance with California law.  
Upon termination, Tenant shall vacate, remove all personal property, return all keys, and leave the Premises in clean condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

* 14. AB 1482 Exemption Notice (Required Statutory Language)
This property is not subject to the rent limits imposed by Section 1947.12 of the Civil Code and is not subject to the just cause requirements of Section 1946.2 of the Civil Code. This property meets the requirements of Sections 1947.12 (d)(5) and 1946.2 (e)(8) of the Civil Code and the owner is not any of the following: (1) a real estate investment trust, as defined by Section 856 of the Internal Revenue Code; (2) a corporation; or (3) a limited liability company in which at least one member is a corporation.

Failure to pay rent when due, violation of any material term after notice to cure (where required), abandonment, or other material breach constitutes default. Landlord’s remedies include those available under California law (three-day notices, unlawful detainer, damages, etc.). All remedies are cumulative.
