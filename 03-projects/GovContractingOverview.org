* Purpose

A small business focused on winning government contracts (federal, state, and local) for the provision of services, with deliberate emphasis on:

- Very low day-to-day operational work
- Preference for multi-year contracts
- Subcontracting the actual performance of work
- Capturing the margin between the government payment and the subcontractor cost

The model prioritizes leverage, paperwork, and relationship management over performing the service itself.

* Core Model (High Level)

1. Form a generic-named LLC
2. Register on SAM.gov (free)
3. Identify suitable solicitations (especially those with low or no past-performance requirements)
4. Use AI + historical pricing data (USAspending.gov) to evaluate and price
5. Obtain subcontractor quotes → mark up → submit bid
6. If awarded: manage invoicing and payment flow (government pays prime → prime pays sub)
7. Ongoing effort target: roughly one hour per month per active contract once established

* Relationship to Other Projects

- Strong practical overlap with AI-agent consulting (AI is used heavily for solicitation analysis and proposal drafting)
- Complements the low-intervention / high-leverage orientation of the broader ZHO work
- Distinct from the SaveYourWisdom nonprofit (commercial vs mission-driven)
