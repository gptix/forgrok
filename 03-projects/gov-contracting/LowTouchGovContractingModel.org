#+TITLE: Low-Touch Government Contracting Model
#+NODE-NAME: LowTouchGovContractingModel
#+FILETAGS: :concept:gov-contracting:zho:
#+CREATED: [2026-08-09 Sun]

* Definition

A business model in which a small prime contractor wins multi-year (or multi-year renewable) government service contracts, subcontracts virtually all performance work, and retains a margin while keeping ongoing operational effort extremely low (target ≈ 1 hour/month per active contract after award).

* Core Principles

1. **Prime, don’t perform** — Own the contract relationship with the government; outsource execution.
2. **Generic capacity** — Use a broad LLC name so the same entity can bid across many service categories.
3. **Data over experience** — Historical pricing (USAspending.gov) + AI analysis substitutes for domain expertise in early bids.
4. **Low past-performance threshold** — Prefer solicitations that do not require extensive prior performance records.
5. **Payment flow advantage** — Government pays the prime first; prime then pays the subcontractor.
6. **Time leverage** — Once awarded, the work shifts almost entirely to invoicing and light oversight.

* Enabling Stack (Current Reference)

- SAM.gov registration (free, mandatory)
- sam.gov/opportunities for discovery
- USAspending.gov for pricing intelligence
- Claude / ChatGPT for solicitation digestion and proposal support
- APEX Accelerators for free technical assistance
- Subcontractor network (local to the place of performance)
