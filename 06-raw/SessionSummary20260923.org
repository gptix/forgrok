** Onboard courier (OBC) / hand-carry
Question evolved from "paid to fly internationally to accompany goods through customs" to *legit logistics only*, then *to Japan*.

Legal industry that still exists:
- Onboard courier / merchandise-in-baggage. Passenger flies with *declared* commercial goods. Company files customs. Not a mule pitch.
- Firms: Chapman Freeborn OBC (public courier portal; JFK→NRT case study), time:matters, medical OBC (e.g. Ontime / stem-cell runs).
- Pay: ticket + modest fee, not driveaway-truck money. Short notice, clean record, passport, credit card.
- Japan: US citizens are *not* on a casual e-visa-for-work path; OBC enters as passenger with a staged commercial file. Japan Customs allows commercial goods as accompanied baggage / entrusted goods (託送品) with C-5360 / invoice; simplified path around ¥300,000; above that formal clearance and often an ACP/IOR for non-residents.
- Reject: undeclared suitcase, "say it is gifts", WhatsApp recruiter.

** Russia travel (US citizen)
- No US *ban* on citizen travel. State Department *Level 4 Do Not Travel* (war, wrongful detention, arbitrary enforcement, thin embassy).
- Russia still issues *consular tourist visas* to Americans. United States is *not* on the unified e-visa list (64 other countries as of 2026 expansion).
- Apply via invitation/voucher + MFA form + Russian posts still open in the US (Washington, New York, Houston). SF consulate long closed. Fee on the order of $185 standard.
- No direct US–Russia flights. US cards generally do not work. Cash and prepaid hotels.
- Advisory is not a criminal prohibition; it is not a normal tourist trip.

** Photographing a house from the public street for marketing
- Taking the photo from a public street of what is visible is generally lawful in the US.
- 17 USC 120(a): constructed building visible from a public place may be photographed, distributed, and publicly displayed without the architect's permission.
- Photographer owns copyright in *their* photograph.
- Georgia appropriation / publicity is about a *person's* name and likeness for financial gain, not a typical facade.
- Marketing risk is implied endorsement, false advertising, identifiable people, murals/artwork as separate copyright, GREC rules if advertising a property *for sale* as a licensee.
- Safer: no people, no claim you worked there, no readable address if avoidable.
